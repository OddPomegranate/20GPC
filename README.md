# 20GPC (A 20 Games To Play With Your Mates PC Port)
A port of the Xbox Live Indie Game 20 Games To Play With Your Mates by IndieSkies to PC.
No keyboard support was added so you need 1-4 controllers.
No online multiplayer was added so you need to use [Parsec](https://parsec.app/)

## Who Made This

20 Games To Play With Your Mates was made completely by IndieSkies, this is a port I made with AI assistance from Claude. I put up this recompilation due to the end of Xbox Live Indie Games and not being able to find a reliable way to play this game. This port will be taken down upon IndieSkies' request, or whenever an official PC port comes around.

## Downloading and Running

1. Get the latest build from the [Releases page](../../releases) (newest version on top).
2. Unzip it anywhere on your PC.
3. Run `20GPC.exe`.

No installer or admin rights are needed as it's a portable folder. To update, download the new release and unzip over (or replace) the old folder.
